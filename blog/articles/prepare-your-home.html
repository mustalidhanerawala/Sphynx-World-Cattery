Preparing Your Home for a Sphynx Kitten: The Complete Setup Guide

Learn how to prepare your home for a Sphynx kitten with this complete setup guide. Discover how to create a warm, safe, and kitten-friendly environment, choose the right supplies, maintain proper temperatures, and ensure your new hairless companion settles in comfortably from day one.

Preparing Your Home Before Your Kitten Arrives

Bringing a Sphynx kitten into your life is an exciting, unique experience—but their lack of fur means preparing your home requires a bit more care than standard kitten-proofing.

Sphynx cats are sensitive to temperature changes, prone to skin oil buildup, and naturally high-energy felines that burn through calories quickly. Creating a warm, clean, and stimulating environment before they arrive helps them adjust comfortably and safely.

1. Establish Dedicated Warm Zones

Set up heated cat beds, thermal pads, and soft fleece blankets in warm locations away from cold drafts. Providing several cozy resting areas allows your kitten to regulate its body temperature throughout the day.

2. Maintain a Stable Indoor Climate

Keep indoor temperatures between 68°F and 77°F (20°C–25°C). Avoid placing sleeping areas beneath air conditioning vents, open windows, or drafty doorways where sudden temperature changes can make your kitten uncomfortable.

3. Switch to Ceramic or Stainless Steel Bowls

Replace plastic bowls with ceramic or stainless steel alternatives. Non-porous materials help reduce bacteria buildup and minimize facial acne and chin blackheads that can affect hairless cats.

4. Stock Up on Soft Sweaters and Clothing

Keep several breathable cotton or fleece sweaters available for cooler weather. Choose well-fitting clothing without tight elastic or loose strings that could irritate delicate skin.

5. Build a Gentle Bathing Station

Prepare a dedicated grooming area with a non-slip bath mat, hypoallergenic cat shampoo, microfiber towels, and gentle ear-cleaning supplies. Regular baths every one to two weeks help control natural skin oils.

6. Choose Low-Dust, Unscented Litter

Select dust-free, unscented litter to reduce eye irritation and skin buildup. Paper, walnut, or quality wood-based litters are excellent choices for Sphynx kittens.

7. Install Stable Cat Trees and Scratchers

Sphynx kittens are energetic climbers that need sturdy cat trees and scratching posts. Choose stable, smooth-surfaced equipment that encourages exercise without causing skin scrapes.

8. Cover or Soften Hard Corners

Protect low furniture corners with soft corner guards. Since Sphynx cats lack a protective coat, cushioning sharp edges helps reduce minor bumps and skin injuries during play.

9. Secure Safe Sunlit Basking Spots

Sphynx cats love relaxing in warm sunlight, but prolonged direct exposure through windows may cause sunburn. Use UV-filtering window film or sheer curtains to reduce excessive sun exposure while still allowing natural warmth.

10. Create a Safe Decompression Zone

Prepare a quiet room with food, fresh water, a litter box, toys, and a warm bed before your kitten arrives. Allowing your new companion to explore one room first helps reduce stress and makes the transition into a new home much smoother.

Essential Home Preparation Checklist

  • ✔ Heated bed or thermal blanket
  • ✔ Ceramic or stainless steel bowls
  • ✔ Soft cotton or fleece sweaters
  • ✔ Hypoallergenic grooming supplies
  • ✔ Low-dust, unscented litter
  • ✔ Stable cat tree and scratching post
  • ✔ Protected furniture corners
  • ✔ Safe sunny resting areas
  • ✔ Quiet room for the first few days

Conclusion: Create a Comfortable Start

Preparing your home for a Sphynx kitten is all about balancing warmth, cleanliness, and safety. By creating cozy resting areas, choosing skin-friendly supplies, and eliminating common household hazards, you provide the ideal environment for your kitten to thrive.

Taking the time to prepare in advance helps ensure a smooth, stress-free transition and allows your new velvet companion to feel safe, comfortable, and at home from the very first day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Sphynx kittens are most comfortable in indoor temperatures between 68°F and 77°F (20°C–25°C). Avoid cold drafts and sudden temperature changes.

Soft cotton or fleece sweaters can help keep Sphynx kittens warm during cooler weather, provided they fit properly and do not irritate the skin.

Ceramic or stainless steel bowls are recommended because they are easier to clean and help reduce bacteria that may contribute to skin irritation and acne.

Most Sphynx kittens benefit from a gentle bath every one to two weeks to remove excess skin oils and keep their skin healthy.

A quiet decompression room helps reduce stress and allows your kitten to become familiar with its new surroundings before exploring the rest of the home.
